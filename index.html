Phase 1

Respectful Discussion Norms & Professional Diagnostic Complexity

Time: ~15 Minutes

1. Class Agreement: Respectful Discussion Norms (5 mins)

When discussing psychological health, we must maintain high ethical standards, empathy, and respect.

No Armchair Diagnosing: Never use clinical labels ("narcissist", "bipolar", "OCD") to describe peers, family, or self.
Person-First Language: People are human beings with experiences, not walking medical labels.
Respect & Confidentiality: Keep discussion objective and focused exclusively on fictional cases.

2. Why Formal Diagnosis Requires Qualified Professionals (10 mins)

Key Principles of Clinical Assessment:

  • Extensive Training Required: Formal clinical diagnosis requires licensed psychiatrists or clinical psychologists with years of specialized doctoral training and standardized psychometric protocols.
  • Comprehensive Differential Evaluation: Clinicians must rule out physical medical conditions, neurodevelopmental factors, substance effects, cultural norms, and temporary trauma responses.
  • Danger of Self-Diagnosis & Viral Media: Internet quizzes and social media videos oversimplify symptoms, generating unnecessary anxiety and false self-labeling.
Phase 2

Conceptualization: Traits, Temporary Behaviors & Clinical Patterns

Time: ~15 Minutes

Psychologists distinguish between normal behavioral variation and clinical constructs using three distinct criteria:

1. Temporary Behavior / State

Short-term emotional reactions or behavioral changes triggered by specific situations (e.g., stress before exams, temporary grief).

Transient & Situation-Bound
2. Personality Traits (Normal Spectrum)

Enduring tendencies along continuous dimensions (e.g., high introversion, meticulous organization, high emotional sensitivity) that adapt flexibly across contexts.

Flexible Spectrum & Adaptive
3. Clinical Disorder Concept

Rigid, pervasive, and enduring patterns of inner experience and behavior that deviate markedly from cultural expectations, causing significant distress or functional impairment.

Rigid, Pervasive & Impairing

1. Pervasiveness: The behavior pattern manifests across almost all personal, social, and academic/work settings.

2. Inflexibility: Inability to adapt behavior to changing situational demands.

3. Significant Distress or Impairment: Causes severe subjective suffering or undermines school, relationship, or daily life functioning.

Phase 3

Responsible Fictional Case Analysis Workshop

Time: ~15 Minutes

Team Challenge: Analyzing Patterns WITHOUT Diagnosing

In teams of 4, read the fictional character vignette below. Identify behavioral patterns, context influences, and potential support avenues without applying diagnostic labels.

Fictional Vignette: Lucas

"Lucas experiences intense anxiety about being abandoned by friends. When a friend cancels plans due to illness, Lucas feels overwhelmed, sends dozens of urgent texts, and then completely withdraws for days, feeling intensely empty and distressed. This pattern has recurred across school years and severely affects his peace of mind."

1. Observed Patterns

Heightened abandonment fear, emotional reactivity, social withdrawal.

Focus on describing specific actions (reaction to cancellations) rather than attaching clinical labels.
2. Impact Analysis

Subjective distress & friction in peer relationships.

Meets the criterion of causing significant personal distress and interpersonal friction.
3. Support Avenues

Proactive support & professional guidance.

Recommending counseling, emotion-regulation strategies, and supportive communication.
Phase 4

Tool Critique, Activity 3 Revision & Evaluation

Time: ~15 Minutes

1. Assessment Tool Critique & Activity 3 Finalization (10 mins)

Students finalize their Unit 3 Activity 3 project. Ensure that your assessment tool critique includes a explicit statement regarding why self-report scales cannot diagnose personality disorders and must be paired with professional clinical evaluation.

2. 5-Minute Exit Ticket: Responsible Language & Stigma Reduction

On an exit slip, complete the following reflection:

"Why is it important to replace casual clinical labels (like calling someone 'narcissistic' or 'bipolar') with respectful, behavior-focused descriptions?"

Interactive Plenary Trivia Challenge

Question: What fundamental feature distinguishes a clinical personality disorder concept from normal trait variation or temporary behavioral stress?

Plenary & Responsible Analysis Rubric

Summative Assessment
Criteria Excellent (4 pts) Satisfactory (3 pts) Needs Work (2 pts) Check
1. Professional Boundaries Clearly explains why formal diagnosis requires licensed professionals and refrains from armchair labeling. Acknowledges professional boundaries with minor prompting. Attempts to diagnose individuals inappropriately.
2. Clinical Distinction Accurately differentiates temporary stress, continuous traits, and pervasive distress/impairment. Explains distress and impairment with minor conceptual gaps. Confuses everyday stress with clinical conditions.
3. Anti-Stigma Language Uses respectful, non-stigmatizing, person-first language throughout case analyses and exit ticket. Uses respectful language with occasional casual terms. Uses stigmatizing or casual diagnostic labels.
